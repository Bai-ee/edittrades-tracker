Talk to the engine, not around it.

The EditTrades GPT reads a closed-candle snapshot of BTC, ETH and SOL from the engine before every answer. The engine owns the trade levels (entry, stop, targets, R:R). The GPT's job is to report them, explain them and size them against your wallet. Use it as a second pair of eyes with fixed rules, not as an oracle.

A session, step by step

GUIDE
  1. Open a fresh chat for each session so no old prices carry over.
  2. Send "signals". Read the call line first (GO IN / HOLD / DON'T), then the DATA block at the bottom to confirm the snapshot is current.
  3. If nothing is actionable, send "forming". Note each Confirmation price and Thesis Eliminated price, and set alerts on your chart.
  4. When an alert fires, send "signals" again. Only act if the plan is now ready and the call says GO IN.
  5. Enter at the quoted entry, place the quoted Stop Loss right away, and use the quoted leverage or less (never above max).
  6. Once in, send your position details (entry, size, collateral, leverage, liquidation) to get HOLD / REDUCE / EXIT guidance and an executable protective stop.
  7. Respect the Time Stop: when it passes, ask again and reassess.

Reading an answer

GUIDE
GO IN / HOLD / DON'T
Three percentages that add to 100. GO IN needs at least 65% plus a ready engine plan, entry, confirmation, stop, targets and acceptable wallet risk. Anything less is HOLD or DON'T.
≥ 65% TO ACT
Recommendation class
The engine's 21/200 flag call: GOOD, WATCH, BAD or DATA_UNAVAILABLE, with Supports / Against / Unknown / What changes. The GPT reports it; it does not recompute it.
ENGINE CALL
Plan status
ready = eligible for GO IN at the quoted levels. conditional = wait for the entry condition. rejected = no trade, with a reason code (chase, rr_below_min, room_at_entry…).
TRADE AUTHORITY
Thesis Eliminated
The price where the idea is wrong. For a long it sits at or below the zone low; for a short at or above the zone high. Never inside the zone.
KILL LEVEL
Stop Loss
The executable exit, with a buffer past Thesis Eliminated. Scalp stops are capped at 3% from entry.
EXIT
R:R
Trades need about 3R to TP1 before fees. Under 1R to TP1 is always DON'T.
≥ 3R GROSS
Mark drift
Stops and liquidation hit on the Pyth mark, not the Kraken close. The GPT flags it when the gap is over 10 bps.
CHECK > 10 BPS
DATA block
Every trade answer ends with Generated At and Closed Through. If they are old, or warnings are listed, ask again before acting.
ALWAYS CHECK

Good follow-up questions

GUIDE
Why is the BTC plan rejected?
What would make ETH GOOD instead of WATCH?
What's your bias on SOL right now?
What's happening with the ETH 1h coil?
I'm long BTC, entry 61000, 5x, liquidation 52400, collateral $2000. What should I do?

For an open position, always give entry, size or notional, collateral, leverage and liquidation price. Without them it can't check that your stop clears liquidation.

What not to do

GUIDE
  • Don't ask it to lower the 65% threshold, widen a scalp stop past 3%, or "just give me an entry". NO TRADE is a valid answer.
  • Don't act on an entry the engine didn't produce. If the GPT offers its own level, it must show R:R, $ loss and wallet risk, and it is labeled legacy or provisional.
  • Don't reuse numbers from earlier in the chat. Prices move; ask again so it pulls a fresh snapshot.
  • Don't treat confidence as win odds. It measures how strong the setup looks, not how likely it is to pay.
  • Counter-trend against the 4h is allowed, but size smaller and keep targets inside the next higher-timeframe level.
  • When it asks for a screenshot (visual confirmation), send the chart it names before any GO IN.

How this ties to the tracker

GUIDE

The call tracker records the same engine calls every 30 minutes and scores them against later candles. Until it has enough scored plans, every number there is provisional. Check it before trusting a class: if GOOD calls are losing, that matters more than any single answer in the chat.
