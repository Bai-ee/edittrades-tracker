How a call is made

The data we use and how it is processed
  1. 1
    Data

    Closed candles from Kraken, 1m to 1D, for BTC, ETH and SOL. Live price from Pyth.

  2. 2
    Indicators

    EMA21, EMA200, Stoch RSI and volume, computed on every timeframe.

  3. 3
    Flag finder

    An impulse move, then a tight pause riding the EMA21, on 1m to 4h.

  4. 4
    Checklist

    Seven timeframes are checked. A flag needs 2/7 aligned, and the next timeframe up must agree, to go out with the trade.

  5. 5
    Lock now

    Entry is the breakout, stop is where the flag is invalid, target is the measured move. Valid until price is 1.5 ATR past entry, for 6 candles.

  6. 6
    Scoring

    Every call is scored: 1x ATR in the called direction before 1x ATR against, within 12 candles of the flag timeframe.
